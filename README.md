# The One-Line Cat Joke That Lands

A make.fun one-line entry: a single joke about cats, one line of 1 to 200
characters, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`, where the
competition's sealed scorer reads it. An AI judge then compares entries two
at a time and keeps the funniest one.

Competition: https://make.fun/api/competitions/u-aldy998-the-one-line-cat-joke-that-lands-a7b18262/requirements

## Entry

My cat walked across my keyboard, deleted my presentation, and then sat on the verdict: it was an improvement.
